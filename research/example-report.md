## Reading this honestly

- **`forecast` measures agreement, not accuracy.** None of the 22 forecasts has resolved, so the reference is the report’s own probabilities. A model that disagrees may turn out to be right. Watch `hedge_rate`: answering 0.5 everywhere buys a middling score while saying nothing.
- **The mock entries are not models.** `mock-oracle` answers from the gold label and `mock-weak` answers naively; they exist to prove a perfect and a floor score are both reachable, so the scorer is known to discriminate.
- **`find-sources` scores the query, not the model’s knowledge**, because the retriever is held fixed. Swap `--search` to compare retrievers instead.
- **Prompts are deliberately plain** - no provider-specific formatting, no role-play, no family-specific tricks. A prompt tuned for one model would invalidate the comparison. They are in `research/prompts/` if you want to change them; change them for everyone at once.
